Commit `feat(audio): ...` (same as PDA-T-0084). `audio::Playback::start(PlaybackConfig, on_event)`, `play(samples, rate)` (resampled to the device rate on the feeder thread, fed through an rtrb ring), `stop()` (epoch + drain flag: the next callback empties the ring and writes silence), `output_rms()` (computed in the callback on the samples written), `output_latency()` (backend-reported, to delay the RMS tap by the audible offset), `is_playing()`, events `DeviceReady/Started/Drained`, device watchdog/rebuild. Verified on this box: 19 unit tests (mono->all channels, RMS of written samples, drain empties 4000 queued samples in one callback, integer outputs); hardware test: stop-to-silent-callback 29 ms (Pulse host) / 6 ms (PipeWire host), Started/Drained events, RMS rises to ~0.035 for a 0.05 amplitude tone, reported PipeWire output latency 42.7 ms. PENDING: RMS-vs-audible alignment +/-30 ms needs a loopback/mic measurement; PDA-SP-0007.R5 'Idle within 100 ms of the last sample' to be checked once the app wires Drained.
